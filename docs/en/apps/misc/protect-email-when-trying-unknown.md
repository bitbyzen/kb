# Protect Your Email When Trying Unknown Services

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

When signing up for an unfamiliar or potentially suspicious service, avoid using your real email address. Here are three approaches to protect your privacy.

## 1. Disposable Email Services

The quickest option — no registration required.

| Service | Notes |
|---|---|
| [Temp Mail](https://temp-mail.org) | Popular and easy to use |
| [10 Minute Mail](https://10minutemail.com) | Inbox self-destructs after 10 minutes |
| [Guerrilla Mail](https://guerrillamail.com) | Lets you choose your own username |

Generate a random email address, paste it into the service, and check the temporary inbox for any verification emails. Once you close the tab, the address is gone.

> **Warning:** Do not use these for important accounts. If you get locked out, you won't be able to recover your password since the email no longer exists.

## 2. Plus Addressing (Gmail / Outlook Alias Trick)

Useful when you want to track whether a service sells your data.

If your email is `yourname@gmail.com`, sign up as:

```
yourname+servicename@gmail.com
```

Emails will still arrive in your normal inbox. You can then set up a filter to delete or label them automatically. If you start receiving spam at that specific address, you'll know exactly who shared your data.

## 3. Privacy-Focused Email Relay Services

For a more permanent and private solution:

- **Apple Hide My Email** *(iCloud+ users)* — Generates unique random addresses that forward to your real inbox. You can delete any address at any time.
- **Firefox Relay** — Similar concept, available without Apple devices.
- **ProtonMail** — A privacy-focused email provider as an alternative main address.

## Summary

| Method | Speed | Privacy Level | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| Disposable email | Instant | High | One-time signups |
| Plus addressing | Instant | Medium | Tracking data leaks |
| Email relay / private provider | Setup needed | High | Long-term privacy |

# Understand Voicebox

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Voicebox is a voice cloning tool that generates speech in your own voice from a short recording. It is not the same as VOICEVOX (the Japanese TTS software).

- Uses Alibaba's **Qwen3 TTS** audio AI model
- Free and runs locally — no cloud required
- Works as a Windows app without Python

## How It Works

1. Launch the app.
1. On first run, download the Qwen3 TTS model.
1. Record a few seconds of your voice with a microphone.
1. Create a voice profile.
1. Enter text to generate speech in your voice.

## Audio Quality

- Relatively natural with good intonation
- Pauses naturally at full stops
- May mispronounce some words — review output before use

## Performance

- GPU recommended (e.g. RTX 4070 Ti generates ~17 seconds of audio in ~3 minutes)
- Generation-based, not real-time

## Pros

- Completely free
- Local processing (private)
- Voice cloning from a short sample
- Easy setup

## Cons

- Slow generation
- Occasional mispronunciation
- High-end GPU recommended for comfort
- Risk of misuse for voice deepfakes

## Use Cases

- Narration
- Video and streaming content
- AI voice content
- VTuber voices

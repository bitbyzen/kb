# Enter Quotation Marks on a Keychron Keyboard

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

The **United States-International** keyboard layout treats the `"` key as a dead key — pressing it once does nothing until you press it a second time or follow it with a space. To make `"` type immediately, replace that layout with the plain **US** layout in Windows Settings.

## Steps

1. Press `Win + I` to open **Settings**.

2. Click **Time & language**.

    ![Windows Settings with Time & language highlighted](enter-quotation-marks-01.png)

3. Click **Language & region**.

    ![Time & language page with Language & region highlighted](enter-quotation-marks-03.png)

4. Under **Preferred languages**, find **English (United States)** and click its **...** button.

    ![Language & region page with the ... button on English (United States) highlighted](enter-quotation-marks-06.png)

5. Click **Language options**.

    ![Dropdown menu showing Language options](enter-quotation-marks-07.png)

6. On the Options page, confirm that **United States-International** is listed under **Keyboards**.

    ![Options page showing United States-International under Keyboards](enter-quotation-marks-08.png)

7. Click **Add a keyboard**.

    ![Keyboards section with Add a keyboard button highlighted](enter-quotation-marks-10.png)

8. Select **US** (QWERTY) from the list.

    ![Keyboard list with US QWERTY highlighted](enter-quotation-marks-11.png)

9. Click the **...** button next to **United States-International** and click **Remove**.

    ![... button next to United States-International highlighted](enter-quotation-marks-13.png)

    ![Remove option highlighted in the dropdown](enter-quotation-marks-14.png)

> **Note:** You do not need to restart your PC for the change to take effect.

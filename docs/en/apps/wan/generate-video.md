# Generate a Video

## Steps

1. Click **Generate** in the left sidebar.

2. In the chatbox at the bottom, select **Video** from the left panel.

3. From the **Function** dropdown at the top of the chatbox, choose **Generate**.

4. Select a model.

5. Upload an image or **First** and **Last** frame images as needed.

6. Upload an audio file as needed.

7. Enter a prompt.

8. Open settings.

9. Set the resolution, aspect ratio, and duration.

10. Toggle **Generate with Credits** as needed.

11. Toggle **Enhance Prompt** as needed.

12. Press **Enter** to generate the image.

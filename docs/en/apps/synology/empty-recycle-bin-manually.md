# Empty Recycle Bin Manually

## Steps

1. Log in to Synology DiskStation Manager.
2. Open **Control Panel**.
3. Under **File Sharing**, open **Shared Folder**.
4. Select the **office** folder, then click **Edit**.
5. In the **General** tab, click **Empty Recycle Bin**.
6. When the confirmation dialog appears, click **Yes**.
7. Click **Save** to close the window.

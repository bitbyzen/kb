# Check the IP Address of Your Synology NAS

> **Note:** This guide was generated by [Claude](https://claude.ai) (AI) and has not been fully reviewed by a human. Please verify the steps before following them in a production environment.

Use Synology Assistant to connect to your NAS and find its local IP address via DSM.

---

## Prerequisites

- A smartphone with an authenticator app set up for DSM login approval
- [Synology Assistant](https://www.synology.com/en-global/support/download) installed on your computer

---

## Steps

### Connect via Synology Assistant

1. Open **Synology Assistant** on your computer.
2. Select your NAS from the server list.
3. Click **Connect**.
4. Enter your **ID** and **password** to log in to DSM.
5. Check your phone — you will be prompted to **approve the login**.
6. Approve the login on your phone.

### Find the IP Address in DSM

7. After logging in, open **Control Panel**.
8. Go to **Info Center**.
9. Select the **Network** tab.
10. Check the status of each **LAN** section (`Connected` / `Disconnected`).
11. Note the IP address of the **Connected** LAN — this is your NAS's local IP address.

---

> **Tip:** The IP address shown here is your NAS's local network address (e.g. `192.168.1.100`).
> You can use this to access DSM directly in a browser without QuickConnect.

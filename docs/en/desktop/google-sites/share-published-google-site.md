# Share a Published Google Site

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

If your Google Site's General access is set to **Restricted**, copying and sending the published link alone is not enough. Anyone who receives the link will see a "Permission Denied" or "Request Access" error unless you explicitly add them first.

## Steps

1. Open your site in the Google Sites editor and click the **Share with others** icon (person silhouette with a + sign) in the top right.
2. In the **Add people and groups** field, type your friends' email addresses.
3. In the dropdown next to their names, change the role from **Editor** to **Published viewer**. This lets them view the site but not edit it.
4. Make sure **Notify people** is checked. Optionally, type a short message in the text box.
5. Click **Send**.

Google sends each person an email with your message and a direct link to the site. When they click the link, Google verifies their account against your approved list and grants access.

> **Note:** Friends must sign in with a Google Account (such as a Gmail address) to view a restricted site.

## Sending the Link Later

Once you have added someone to the approved list, you can click **Copy published site link** at any time and share the URL directly — for example, in a group chat. Anyone already on your approved list will be let in automatically.

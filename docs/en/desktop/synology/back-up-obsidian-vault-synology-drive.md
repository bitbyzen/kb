# Back Up Your Obsidian Vault with Synology Drive

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Synology Drive is the recommended tool for backing up a specific folder like your Obsidian Vault. This guide walks you through the full setup.

---

## Step 1: Install Synology Drive Server on Your NAS

1. Log into DSM (your NAS web interface).
2. Open **Package Center**.
3. Search for **Synology Drive Server**.
4. Click **Install**.

This turns your NAS into a private cloud, similar to Google Drive.

---

## Step 2: Enable Your Home Folder

1. Go to **Control Panel**.
2. Open **User & Group**.
3. Go to the **Advanced** tab.
4. Enable **User Home Service**.

This creates a personal folder for your account (usually `/home/Drive`).

---

## Step 3: Install Synology Drive Client on Windows 11

1. Download **Synology Drive Client** from the Synology website.
2. Install it on your PC.
3. Launch the app.

---

## Step 4: Connect to Your NAS

1. Click **Start Now**.
2. Enter your NAS IP address (e.g., `192.168.x.x`), username, and password.
3. Click **Next**.

If you are on the same local network, the connection is usually straightforward.

---

## Step 5: Choose "Backup Task"

When prompted, select **Backup Task**, not Sync Task.

> **Note:** Backup Task is safer for Obsidian. It is a one-way upload (PC to NAS) and avoids sync conflicts.

---

## Step 6: Select Your Obsidian Vault Folder

Set the local and remote folders:

**Local folder (on your PC):**

```
C:\Users\<username>\Documents\Obsidian Vault
```

**Remote folder (on your NAS):**

```
/home/Drive/Obsidian Backup
```

You can rename the remote folder if you prefer.

---

## Step 7: Set the Backup Mode

Choose one of the following:

- **Continuous backup** — backs up automatically whenever files change (recommended for Obsidian)
- **Scheduled backup** — runs at a fixed interval (e.g., hourly or daily)

Use **Continuous backup** for Obsidian to keep your notes protected in near real-time.

---

## Step 8: Enable Versioning

Turn on **File versioning** and set a retention count such as 10–20 versions.

> **Note:** Versioning lets you recover notes that were deleted, overwritten, or corrupted.

---

## Step 9: Finish Setup

1. Review your settings.
2. Click **Apply**.

Your Obsidian Vault is now automatically backed up to your Synology NAS.

---

## Tips for Obsidian

**Do:**

- Use Backup Task, not Sync Task.
- Keep versioning on.

**Avoid:**

- Syncing the same vault with multiple tools at the same time.
- Using two-way sync unless you fully understand how it handles conflicts.

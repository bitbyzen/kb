## 1. Recommerce (リコマース)

**Website:** [recommerce.co.jp](https://www.recommerce.co.jp/)

A mail-in buyback service powered by Amazon Japan, operated by Recommerce Co., Ltd. Sell your unwanted items and get paid in Amazon Gift Cards — entirely free of charge.

### Key Features

- **Free service** — boxes, pickup, shipping, appraisal, and returns all free
- **Easy signup** — uses your existing [Amazon Japan account](https://www.amazon.co.jp/)
- **Fast payment** — Amazon Gift Cards issued immediately after appraisal
- **Average payout** — approx. ¥6,067 per order (2023–2024)
- **First-time bonus** — [30% UP coupon for new users](https://www.recommerce.co.jp/campaign/first/)

## 2. セゾンの買取り (Saison Kaitori)

**Website:** [kaitori.creditsaison.jp](https://kaitori.creditsaison.jp/)

An at-home appraisal and buyback service run jointly by Credit Saison and BuySell Technologies. A professional appraiser visits your home to evaluate and purchase items on the spot.

### Key Features

- **Pre-appraisal tool** — "おうちのお宝 総診断!!" self-assessment before booking
- **Home visit** — professional appraiser comes to you for on-site valuation and purchase
- **8-day return guarantee** after transaction
- **Support hours** — Phone: 9:00–18:00 (year-round except New Year); Web: 24/7
- **App access** — via セゾン Portal app (Saison Card holders) or UC Portal app (UC Card holders)

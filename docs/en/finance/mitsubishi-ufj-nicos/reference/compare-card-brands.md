# Understand Mitsubishi UFJ Card vs. DC Card

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

## Background: Multiple Brands, One Company

Mitsubishi UFJ NICOS has undergone multiple mergers over the years, resulting in several card brands coexisting under one company. Think of them as different product lines from the same parent organization.

| Brand | Positioning |
|---|---|
| **Mitsubishi UFJ Card** | Current flagship brand. The latest series the company is focusing on most going forward. |
| **DC Card** | Rooted in the former Diamond Credit lineage. A traditional brand with a loyal following. |
| **MUFG Card** | Former flagship prior to 2021. Currently being transitioned to "Mitsubishi UFJ Card." |
| **NICOS Card** | Rooted in the former Nippon Shinpan lineage. Continuing as a familiar, approachable brand. |

## Should You Switch to the Mitsubishi UFJ Card?

If you are not particularly attached to your DC Card, the Mitsubishi UFJ Card offers several modern advantages:

- **Higher rewards rate** — Up to 15% points back at participating stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, Matsuya, etc.). Conditions apply.
- **Numberless card design** — No card number printed on the card face, providing improved security.
- **Improved app experience** — A modern UI makes tracking spending and managing your account easier.

> **Warning:** Your DC Card will not automatically convert to a Mitsubishi UFJ Card. If you wish to switch, you must apply for a new card. You can then choose to cancel your DC Card or keep both.

## Why Does My App Show "Mitsubishi UFJ Card" for My DC Card?

This is a common source of confusion. There are three reasons this happens.

### Reason 1: The App Name Was Unified

Previously, each brand had its own app (e.g., "DC Card App"). These have been consolidated into a single **"Mitsubishi UFJ Card App"** that manages all brands (DC, MUFG, NICOS, and Mitsubishi UFJ Card). As a result, the label "Mitsubishi UFJ Card" appears throughout the app as an umbrella term.

### Reason 2: Card Names Are Relabeled in the App

The app's card list and registration screens display all cards under the collective service name "Mitsubishi UFJ Card" rather than individual brand names. Your specific card is still correctly linked — confirm this by checking the last four digits of the card number.

| Context | Displayed As |
|---|---|
| Your physical card | DC Card |
| Mitsubishi UFJ Card App display | Mitsubishi UFJ Card (umbrella service name) |
| Apple Pay / Google Wallet | Mitsubishi UFJ Card (branding shown on-screen) |

### Reason 3: Same Applies to Apple Pay and Google Wallet

Even if your DC Card is registered in a mobile wallet (e.g., Apple Pay), the wallet screen will display the "Mitsubishi UFJ Card" branding. This is the current standard behavior across all Mitsubishi UFJ NICOS platforms.

## Summary

- DC Card and Mitsubishi UFJ Card are separate products under the same company.
- Your DC Card remains valid and unchanged — no automatic conversion occurs.
- The app and digital wallets showing "Mitsubishi UFJ Card" is a branding/display convention, not an error.
- To get the newer card's benefits (higher rewards, numberless design), you must apply separately.

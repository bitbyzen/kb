# Back Up Photos to PhotoLibrary Only

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

> **Note:** This procedure prevents `MobileBackup` and year/month subfolders from being created automatically, saving all photos flat under `PhotoLibrary`.

1. Open the **Synology Photos** app on your phone.
2. Tap the **More** tab at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap the area showing **"Image backup complete"** (or the current backup status).
4. Tap the **gear icon (Settings)** in the top right.
5. Tap **Backup destination**.
6. Under **Destination path**, select **Customize** and set the path to **`PhotoLibrary`**.
7. Turn off the **Auto-create year/month folders** toggle.

> **Note:** With this setting, photos are saved flat under `PhotoLibrary` rather than in device- or date-based subfolders. This workflow is well suited to manually sorting large photo collections from your PC.

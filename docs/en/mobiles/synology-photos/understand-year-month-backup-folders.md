# Understand Why Mobile Backup Creates Year and Month Folders

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

> **Note:** This procedure is based on Pixel 8a (Android).

## The Folder Structure Is Built In

Synology Photos always organizes mobile backups by device, year, and month. Files land at:

```text
/home/Photos/MobileBackup/<device-name>/<year>/<month>/
```

This applies to every backup source you select — Camera, Screenshots, or any custom folder. There is no setting in Synology Photos to disable the year/month subfolders or back up into a flat folder instead.

## Workarounds

If the subfolders bother you, consider one of these approaches instead of fighting the built-in structure:

- **Browse with Timeline view.** Synology Photos shows all backed-up images in chronological order regardless of their folder location, so you never need to look at the year/month subfolders during normal use.
- **Use a third-party sync app.** Apps such as FolderSync (Android) can sync a folder on your phone directly to a NAS share over SMB or WebDAV, giving you full control over the destination path — including a flat one.
- **Run a periodic flattening script.** Set up a script in DSM Task Scheduler that moves files out of the year/month subfolders into the parent folder and removes the empty subfolders afterward.

> **Note:** Option one requires no extra setup and works well for most people. Options two and three replace or supplement the built-in backup and need more configuration.

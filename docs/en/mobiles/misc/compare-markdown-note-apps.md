## Verdict

**Choose UpNote** if you want rich formatting, notebooks, note locking, and a polished writing experience — and you don't mind paying a small fee.

**Choose Simplenote** if you want fast, distraction-free plain-text notes that are 100% free, sync instantly, and work everywhere with zero setup.

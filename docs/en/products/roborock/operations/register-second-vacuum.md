# Register a Second Vacuum Cleaner

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Applies to: Roborock S6 MaxV Robot Vacuum Cleaner.

## Step 1: Add a Second Device to the Roborock App

1. Open the product cover.
2. Press and hold the **Spot Cleaning** button and the **Dock Station** button simultaneously to activate pairing mode.
3. The blue indicator light will begin blinking slowly.
4. Open the Roborock app on your phone.
5. Tap the **+** (Add) button to register the device.
6. Your vacuum cleaner will appear at the top of the screen.
7. Tap the cleaner to navigate to the next screen.
8. Select your Wi-Fi network.

    > **Note:** This product only supports the 2.4 GHz band. However, if only your 5 GHz network is listed on the screen, follow the steps below.

9. Select your 5 GHz network (e.g. `Buffalo-A-XXXX`).
10. Edit the label to reflect the correct band (e.g. change `Buffalo-A-XXXX` to `Buffalo-G-XXXX`).

    > **Note:** You can edit the label of the 5 GHz network.

11. Follow the on-screen instructions.
12. Registration is complete.

## Step 2: Use the Second Device

1. Open the app.
2. Tap **Device** at the bottom.
3. Swipe left or right to select the desired device.

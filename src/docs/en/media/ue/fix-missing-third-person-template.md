# Fixing the Missing Third Person Template

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

The **New Project** dialog shows only **Blank**, even under the **Games** tab — Third Person, First Person, Top Down, and the other templates are missing.

## Cause

This isn't a UI issue — the Third Person template itself isn't installed on your machine. In the Epic Games Launcher install, template and starter-content packs are optional components that can get skipped or dropped during install or update. When that happens, the templates disappear from the **New Project** dialog.

## Solution

1. Open the **Epic Games Launcher** and go to your **Library**. Find your Unreal Engine version.
2. Click the dropdown arrow (or gear/options icon) next to **Launch** and choose **Options**.
3. Make sure **Templates and Feature Packs** is checked (sometimes labeled as additional feature/target packs), then click **Verify** (or **Update**) to redownload the missing files.
4. Once it finishes, relaunch Unreal Engine and open **New Project** → **Games** tab again. Third Person, First Person, Top Down, Vehicle, and the other templates should now appear alongside Blank.

## If That Doesn't Work

If **Verify** doesn't restore the templates, a full uninstall and reinstall of the engine is the more forceful fallback.

> **Note:** Your projects are safe during this — they're stored separately in your `Unreal Projects` folder, not inside the engine install.

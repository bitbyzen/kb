# Using a Pen Tablet in Blender

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Blender works well with pen tablets. The pen tip acts as a left click, and these tips cover the rest of what a mouse normally does.

## Move Things with the Gizmo

Click the **Move** tool in the left toolbar (the cross-shaped arrows icon). Arrows appear on your selection, and you can drag an arrow with the pen — for example, the blue (Z) arrow to move straight up. Nothing moves until you drag, and you can fine-tune afterward in the **Move** panel at the bottom-left of the viewport.

## Navigate Without a Mouse

At the top-right of the viewport, drag the colored axis ball to orbit, the hand icon to pan, and the magnifier to zoom. See [Orbiting and Panning the View](orbit-and-pan-view.md).

## Orbit with Alt

Go to **Edit** → **Preferences** → **Input** and turn on **Emulate 3 Button Mouse**. Then `Alt` + pen drag orbits the view.

## Map the Pen's Side Buttons

Your tablet driver can map the pen's side buttons to middle click (orbit) or right click.

## Try Sculpt Mode for Shaping

For organic reshaping, Sculpt Mode can feel more natural with a pen than Edit Mode. Switch from Edit Mode to Sculpt Mode using the top-left dropdown, choose the **Grab** brush, set a small radius in the header, and turn on X symmetry (the butterfly icon). Then drag as if pushing clay — see [Sculpting with Clay Strips, Smooth, and Grab](sculpt-clay-strips-smooth-grab.md).

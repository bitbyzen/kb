# Comparing MobileBackup and PhotoLibrary in Synology Photos

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Synology Photos separates automatic backup and manual organisation into two distinct folders under `home` → `Photos`.

## MobileBackup — Automatic Backup

Photos uploaded automatically from your phone are stored here. When you enable backup in the app, a subfolder is created for each device (e.g. iPhone, Pixel), and photos are saved inside by date.

- Purpose: "Keep a copy of phone photos automatically"

## PhotoLibrary — Manual Organisation

Photos uploaded manually from a PC via the browser, or folders you create yourself for organisation, go here.

- Purpose: "Manage and organise your main photo library"

## Recommended Workflow

1. Let `MobileBackup` receive automatic uploads from your phone.
1. Periodically move your favourite or important photos into organised folders inside `PhotoLibrary`.

On DS720+ (SSD configuration), both folders are displayed together in the **Timeline** view in chronological order, so the split location does not affect how you browse photos.

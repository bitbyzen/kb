# Back Up Smartphone Photos with Synology Photos

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Install Synology Photos on both DSM and your phone to automatically back up photos to your NAS.

---

## Install Synology Photos on DSM

1. Log in to **Synology DiskStation Manager (DSM)**.
2. Open **Package Center**.
3. Search for **Synology Photos** and click **Install**.

---

## Choose a Backup Space

Two backup spaces are available. Choose one based on your use case:

| Space | Best for | Save destination |
|-------|----------|-----------------|
| **Personal Space** | Personal use — only you can view and manage photos | `/home/Photos/MobileBackup/[your phone name]` |
| **Shared Space** | Family or group use — share albums with others | `/photo/MobileBackup/[your phone name]` |

### Enable Personal Space (required for Personal Space)

Personal Space requires the **User Home** service to be enabled first:

1. Open **Control Panel**.
2. Go to **User & Group** → **Advanced**.
3. Scroll down to **User Home**.
4. Enable **Enable user home service**.

---

## Set Up on Your Phone

1. Install **Synology Photos** on your phone.

    - [App Store (iOS)](https://apps.apple.com/app/synology-photos/id1474764070)
    - [Google Play (Android)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.synology.photo)

2. Log in with your:

    - **QuickConnect ID** (or NAS IP address)
    - **Account** (your DSM username)
    - **Password**

3. Tap **Enable Photo Backup** when prompted.

    > **Note:** The **Enable Photo Backup** button appears only on first login.
    > If you missed it, go to **Me** → **Photo Backup** to enable it manually.

---

## Next Steps

- Open Synology Photos on your phone and confirm the first backup has completed.
- On DSM, browse to your backup destination folder to verify photos are arriving.
- Adjust backup settings (Wi-Fi only, video backup, etc.) under **Me** → **Photo Backup** → **Settings**.

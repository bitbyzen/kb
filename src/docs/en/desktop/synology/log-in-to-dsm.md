# Logging In to DSM

## Steps

1. Prepare your phone.
1. Launch **Synology Assistant** on your PC.
1. Select the server.

    > **Note:** If no servers are displayed, go to [find.synology.com](http://find.synology.com) or enter the NAS's IP address directly in the browser's address bar.

1. Click **Connect**.
1. Your browser opens.
1. Enter your ID when prompted.
1. Check your phone.
1. Approve the sign-in request in **Secure SignIn**.

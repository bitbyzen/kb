# Log In to DSM

## Steps

1. Prepare your phone.
2. Launch **Synology Assistant** on your PC.
3. Select the server.

   > **Note:** If no servers are displayed, go to [find.synology.com](http://find.synology.com) or enter the NAS's IP address directly in the browser's address bar.

4. Click **Connect**.
5. Your browser opens.
6. Enter your ID when prompted.
7. Check your phone.
8. Approve the sign-in request in **Secure SignIn**.

# Adding a New Profile on Meta Quest 3

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Meta Quest 3 supports up to **3 additional profiles** alongside the primary account (4 accounts total), making it easy to share your headset with household members while keeping saves and settings separate.

## Prerequisites

Before you begin, the person you're adding must:

- Have their own **Meta account**. They can create one at [meta.com](https://www.meta.com) or through the Meta Horizon mobile app.
- Have the **Meta Horizon app** installed on their phone and logged in to their account.

## Steps

1. Put on the headset and press the **Meta button** on your right controller to open the universal menu.
2. Hover over the **clock icon** on the left side of the menu. When Quick Settings appears, select it.
3. In the top-right corner of Quick Settings, select **Settings**.
4. Select **Accounts**, then select **Add Account** in the top-right corner.
5. An **8-digit code** will appear on the headset screen.
6. On the new person's phone, open a browser and go to **[meta.com/device](https://www.meta.com/device)**, then enter the 8-digit code.
7. Pass the headset to the new person (or continue yourself). They will complete initial setup on the headset, including:
    - Logging in to their Meta account
    - Adjusting headset fit
    - Setting up Guardian (the boundary system)

## Switch Between Profiles

1. Press the **Meta button** to open the universal menu.
2. Select your **profile picture** on the far left.
3. Select **Switch Accounts** in the top-right of the profile screen.
4. Choose the account to switch to.

## Enable App Sharing

By default, additional profiles can only access apps they have purchased themselves. The primary account holder can enable **App Sharing** to let all profiles access their purchased apps:

1. Go to **Settings** → **Accounts**.
2. Enable **App Sharing**.

## Notes

- Additional profiles can save their own app progress, achievements, and leaderboard scores independently.
- The primary account cannot be removed without performing a factory reset on the headset.
- To remove a profile, go to **Settings** → **Accounts**, find the profile, and select the remove option. Locally saved data for that profile will be deleted (some games may retain cloud saves on that user's Meta account).

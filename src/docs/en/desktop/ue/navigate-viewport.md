# Navigating Viewport

The Viewport is the panel where you view and edit your level.

## Look Around

1. Hold the **right mouse button**.
2. Move the mouse.

The camera rotates in the direction you move the mouse.

---

## Move the Camera

1. Hold the **right mouse button**.
2. Press the following keys:

| Key | Action        |
| --- | ------------- |
| W   | Move forward  |
| S   | Move backward |
| A   | Move left     |
| D   | Move right    |
| Q   | Move down     |
| E   | Move up       |

---

## Move Faster or Slower

Hold the **right mouse button**, then use:

* **Mouse wheel up** — Move faster
* **Mouse wheel down** — Move slower

---

## Move While Holding Middle Mouse Button (Optional)

Hold the **middle mouse button**, then move the mouse.

The camera pans left, right, up, or down.

---

## Move and Turn with the Left Mouse Button (Optional)

1. Hold the **left mouse button**.
2. Move the mouse forward or back to move the camera forward or backward.
3. Move the mouse left or right to turn the camera left or right.

---

## Pan with Both Mouse Buttons (Optional)

Hold the **left and right mouse buttons together**, then move the mouse to slide the camera without rotating it. This is the same pan as holding the middle mouse button.

> **Note:** These controls also apply in the Skeletal Mesh Editor (Persona) and Static Mesh Editor's preview viewports, not just the main Level Editor viewport.

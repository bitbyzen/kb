# Removing a PC from the Microsoft Account
1. Click **Linked devices**.
![Screenshot 01](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-01.webp)
1. Click **Remove device**. 
![Screenshot 02](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-02.webp)
1. Click **Remove this device**.
![Screenshot 03](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-03.webp)
1. Select **I'm ready to remove this device**, then click **Remove**.
![Screenshot 04](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-04.webp)
1. Click **Remove from Microsoft Store**.
![Screenshot 05](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-05.webp)
1. Click **Unlink**.
![Screenshot 06](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-06.webp)
1. Click **Unlink**.
![Screenshot 07](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-07.webp)
1. Click **Close**.
![Screenshot 07](img/remove-pc-from-microsoft-account-08.webp)

# Generate an Image

## Steps

1. Click **Generate** in the left sidebar.

2. In the chatbox at the bottom, select **Image** from the left panel.

3. From the **Function** dropdown at the top of the chatbox, choose **Generate**.

4. Upload a reference image as needed.

5. Enter your prompt.

6. Select a model.

7. Open **Settings**.

8. Set the desired aspect ratio.

9. Toggle **Generate with Credits** as needed.

10. Toggle **Enhance Prompt** as needed.

11. Press **Enter** to generate the image.

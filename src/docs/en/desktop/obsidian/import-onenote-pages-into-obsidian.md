# Importing OneNote Pages into Obsidian

Use Obsidian’s **Importer** plugin to pull your notes straight from **Microsoft OneNote** into your Obsidian vault in Markdown format.

Official reference: [https://help.obsidian.md/plugins/importer](https://help.obsidian.md/plugins/importer)

## Step 1: Install the Importer Plugin

1. Open **Settings**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-01.webp)

2. Open **Community plugins**, then disable **Restricted mode**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-02.webp)

3. Click **Browse**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-03.webp)

4. Search for **Importer**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-04.webp)

5. Click **Install**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-05.webp)

6. Click **Enable**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-06.webp)

## Step 2: Sign In with Your Microsoft Account

1. Click **Open Importer**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-07.webp)

2. Select **Microsoft OneNote**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-08.webp)

3. Sign in with your Microsoft account.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-09.webp)

4. Click **Continue**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-10.webp)

## Step 3: Import OneNote Notebooks

1. Select notebooks to import, then click **Import**.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-11.webp)

2. The plugin converts pages to Markdown and saves attachments in your vault.

    ![Screenshot](img/import-onenote-pages-into-obsidian-12.webp)

## Notes

* Only notebooks synced to OneDrive are supported.
* Large notebooks may take time to process.
* Complex layouts, ink drawings, and embedded files may not convert perfectly.
* Review imported notes and adjust formatting if necessary.

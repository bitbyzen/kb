# Displaying Your Windows Screen on Meta Quest 3

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Using the official **Windows App + Mixed Reality Link** solution by Microsoft, generally available as of October 2025.

## Requirements

- Meta Quest 3 or Quest 3S (on Horizon OS v81 or later)
- Windows 11 PC
- Both devices on the same Wi-Fi network
- A Microsoft work or school account (**personal accounts are not supported**)

## Step 1: Set Up Your PC

1. On your Windows 11 PC, open the **Microsoft Store**.
2. Search for and install **Mixed Reality Link**.
3. Launch the app — it runs in the background and makes your PC discoverable by the headset.

## Step 2: Install Windows App on Your Headset

1. Put on your Quest 3 and open the **Meta Horizon Store**.
2. Search for **"Windows App"** and install it.
3. Launch the Windows App from your headset library.

## Step 3: Connect

1. In the Windows App, sign in with your **Microsoft work or school account**.
2. Select **"PC near me"** from the menu.
3. A pairing code will appear on your PC screen.
4. Look at your keyboard from within the headset and tap **Pair** to confirm.

## Key Features

- Up to **3 virtual monitors** in your mixed reality environment
- Supports both **local PC** and **Windows 365 Cloud PC** connections
- Also connects to **Azure Virtual Desktop** and **Microsoft Dev Box**
- Passthrough mode lets you stay aware of your physical surroundings

## Troubleshooting

- **Can't find your PC?** Make sure Mixed Reality Link is running and both devices are on the same network.
- **Pixelated display?** Check for network latency — a wired Ethernet connection on your PC helps significantly.
- **Personal Microsoft account not working?** Work or school accounts only; `@outlook.com` addresses are not supported.
- **Pairing fails?** Restart both the headset and Mixed Reality Link, then try again.

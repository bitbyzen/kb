# Installing Google Finance on Google Pixel

> **Note:** This guide was generated by ChatGPT and has not been fully reviewed by a human.

## Use Chrome (Recommended)

1. Open Google Chrome.
2. Go to Google Finance (search for “Google Finance” and open the site).
3. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top-right corner.
4. Tap **“Install app.”**

   * If this option is not available, tap **“Add to Home screen”** instead.
5. Tap **“Install”** to confirm.

---

## Finish Setup

* Locate the new icon on your home screen.
* Tap it to open Google Finance.
* It will launch in full-screen mode like a standard app.

---

## Optional: Create a Shortcut for a Specific Stock

1. Open a stock page (e.g., Arm Holdings).
2. Tap the three-dot menu (⋮).
3. Tap **“Add to Home screen.”**

A shortcut will be created that opens directly to that stock.

---

## Notes

* This is not an app from the Play Store.
* It is a Progressive Web App (PWA).
* It is safe and officially supported by Google.

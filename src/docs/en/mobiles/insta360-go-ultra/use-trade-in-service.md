# Use the Insta360 Trade-In Service

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

The [Insta360 Trade-In service](https://www.insta360.com/support/workorder/trade-in/create) lets you exchange your old or unused camera for a discount coupon of up to US$40 toward a new Insta360 product.

## Eligible Devices

You can trade in cameras from other brands, including:

| Brand | Models |
|-------|--------|
| GoPro | HERO, MAX |
| DJI | Osmo Pocket, Osmo Action, Osmo Mobile, OM |

Insta360 devices are also accepted.

## How It Works

**Step 1: Apply online**

Go to the [Trade-In page](https://www.insta360.com/support/workorder/trade-in/create) and submit your device details.

**Step 2: Ship for free**

Return your old device using the prepaid shipping label provided.

**Step 3: Get your coupon**

A coupon is issued for the new Insta360 model you choose and can be applied at checkout on the Insta360 Store.

## Notes

- Coupon value is up to US$40 and applies to specific latest Insta360 models.
- Insta360 performs secure data-wiping on all received devices.
- To check the status of an existing trade-in, visit the [Trade-In progress page](https://www.insta360.com/support/workorder/trade-in/create).

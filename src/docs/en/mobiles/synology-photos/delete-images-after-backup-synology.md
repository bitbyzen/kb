# Deleting Images After Backup in Synology Photos

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

1. Open the **Synology Photos** app on your phone.
2. Tap the **More** tab at the bottom of the screen.
3. Tap the area showing **"Image backup complete"**.
4. Tap **Free up space**.
5. Confirm that **All backed-up items** is selected.
6. Tap **Clear all** at the bottom of the screen.
7. Tap **Allow** on the OS confirmation popup.

> **Note:** Only files that have been fully backed up to the NAS are deleted. On Pixel 8a, deleted files may be moved to the **Google Photos** trash. To free up storage immediately, empty the Google Photos trash manually.

# Understanding Why SpaceDesk Is Free

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

SpaceDesk is free for personal use because the company behind it, datronicsoft Technology GmbH, earns revenue from enterprise and OEM licensing. The free version serves as both a product and a showcase of their display virtualization technology.

## Trade-Offs vs. Paid Alternatives

| | SpaceDesk | SuperDisplay |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | Paid |
| Wireless display | Yes | Yes |
| Device support | Broad | Focused |
| Pen experience | Varies | Polished for artists |
| Setup | May require tweaking | Generally simpler |

## Known Limitations

- Wireless latency depends on your network quality.
- Stylus support is less refined than dedicated drawing solutions.
- Updates can occasionally introduce issues.
- Support is limited compared with commercial creative software.

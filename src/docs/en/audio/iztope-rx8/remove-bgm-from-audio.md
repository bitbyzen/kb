# Removing BGM from Audio

> **Note:** This document was generated by Claude Code and has not been reviewed by a human. Please use it as a reference only.

Use iZotope RX8's **Music Rebalance** module to isolate vocals by removing or reducing background music.

## Step 1: Open the Audio File

1. Launch iZotope RX8.
2. Drag and drop an audio file onto the application window.

## Step 2: Open Music Rebalance

1. In the module list on the right side of the screen, click **Music Rebalance**.

## Step 3: Isolate the Vocals

1. To keep only the vocals, lower the faders for all non-vocal elements (bass, drums, other instruments).
2. Alternatively, click the **S** (solo) icon on the **Vocals** fader to mute all other elements at once.

> **Warning:** Do not raise the Vocals fader above its default level. Boosting the vocal volume risks clipping in the output file.

## Step 4: Set the Quality

- **Good** (default) — faster processing, suitable for most use cases.
- **Best** — higher quality but significantly longer render time.

## Step 5: Preview and Compare

1. Click **Preview** to hear your current settings.
2. Click **Compare** to open the Compare Settings dialog.
   - Select **Original audio** to hear the unprocessed file.
   - Select **Music Rebalance: Settings 1** to hear your adjusted version.

## Step 6: Export the File

1. When you are satisfied with the result, click **Render** to export the processed audio file.

# Generating a Video with an Original Character

## Steps

1. Click **Generate** in the left sidebar.

2. In the chatbox at the bottom, select **Video** from the left panel.

3. From the **Function** dropdown at the top of the chatbox, choose **Starling**.

4. Click **Add Role**.

5. Click **Upload Video**.

6. Upload a video that is longer than 5 seconds.

7. Trim the video to a 5-second segment.

8. Select the registered character.

9. Enter your prompt.

10. Press **Enter** to generate the video.

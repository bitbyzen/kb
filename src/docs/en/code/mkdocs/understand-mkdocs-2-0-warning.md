## What's Happening

MkDocs 2.0 is a new major version of the underlying framework, but the Material for MkDocs team is warning that it's essentially incompatible with the current ecosystem:

- The plugin system is being removed, so any plugins you use will break
- The theming system is being rewritten, so Material's theme itself won't work
- There's no migration path from MkDocs 1.x projects
- It's currently unlicensed and has a closed contribution model

## What You Should Do (For Now): Nothing

This is a warning about a future version, not something that's broken today. Your current Docker setup with Material for MkDocs is fine. The team is essentially signaling they won't be supporting MkDocs 2.0, and advising users not to upgrade when it releases.

## Longer Term

- Stay on **MkDocs 1.x** with Material for MkDocs for the foreseeable future
- Watch the Material for MkDocs blog/repo — they may announce an alternative path or their own fork
- Pin your MkDocs version in your `Dockerfile` or `requirements.txt` (e.g. `mkdocs>=1.5,<2.0`) to avoid accidentally pulling in 2.0
